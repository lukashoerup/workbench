# Which version of each frame was chosen, and why

Seventeen of the twenty-four shots exist in more than one version. Assembling
the set by "newest wins" is not selection — it silently replaced two frames
with worse ones. This is the record of what was actually compared and picked.

## Corrections, where the newer version fixed a fact

Judged on correctness first. In each of these the earlier version was simply
wrong about the world, and the replacement was checked to be no worse in craft:

| Shot | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| S1-02 | Children at a **burnt-out** fire, no bicycle | The fire burning as in the shot before, bicycle dropped on the track |
| S1-05 | **Yellow and black** hazard tape | Blue and white Danish police tape |
| S2-04 | Vials on a light box | Dental X-ray films, teeth readable as shapes |
| S3-02 | A wall of **motor oil** | One row of clear bottles with a gap in it |
| S3-05 | A **log** | A poured plaster cast with the tread in reverse |
| S4-05 | Wood **already alight**, in the shot that exists to show it before | Cold, unlit, torchlit |
| S5-03 | A **readable profile** — a rails breach | A silhouette with no feature readable |

## Judgement calls, where both versions were defensible

**S2-01 — changed.** Four versions. The one that had been assembled in showed a
turquoise carpet, off-palette, in a centred composition. Chosen instead: the
version with a thin surgical glove rather than a heavy rubber gauntlet, correct
palette, and depth shallow enough that the room falls away as the brief asks.
*This is the one the "newest wins" rule got wrong.*

**S3-01 — changed.** Two versions, both with the fire burning behind. Chosen the
one where the bottle lies on its side with one flank visibly melted and slumped,
because the melting is the point — it is what tells you the bottle was in the
fire.

**S3-04 — kept.** Three versions. The newest has a slightly finer tread, closer
to a passenger car, but the earlier frame is the stronger picture by a distance:
better raking light, better rain, more depth. Neither is a convincing VW Golf
tread. **Noted as an open craft issue rather than resolved by swapping** — a
marginal factual gain is not worth a clear loss in the image.

## The rule this establishes

Never assemble a set by recency. Compare like for like, and write down why.
A correction that fixes a fact still has to be checked for what it cost
elsewhere in the frame.
