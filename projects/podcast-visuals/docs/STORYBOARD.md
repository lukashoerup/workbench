# Storyboard — the five segments

Lasse named five timecodes and asked to see a frame for each. Each one gets a
**hero frame** — that frame — plus the shots around it, because one frame
proves the look and a sequence proves the format.

22 shots, 108 seconds of picture. The exact prompts are in
[PROMPT-PACK.md](PROMPT-PACK.md), generated from the same data.

**One caveat, stated once.** The narration has not been transcribed — the audio
has not reached a machine that can read it. Every `subject` line below is built
from Lasse's own note on that timecode, not from what is actually said. When
the audio arrives, the subject lines get rewritten against the transcript and
everything else — look, grammar, camera language, rails — stands unchanged.
Expect the shot *content* to move and the shot *craft* not to.

## 03:30–06:00 · children, the bonfire, the response
*"legede børn og bål og beredskab på gerningsstedet"*

The job of this sequence is to make an ordinary place ordinary first. If the
audience sees a crime scene in the first frame, the turn is gone.

| | Shot | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **★ S1-01** | **The unlit stack on the field edge, a child's bicycle on its side in the grass. Hip height, 35mm, flat pre-rain light.** | **B, 6s** |
| S1-02 | Three children at fifty metres, silhouettes only, running past towards the treeline. 85mm, compressed | B |
| S1-03 | The burnt-out ring next morning — white ash, blackened timber ends, thin smoke. 50mm, low and close | B |
| S1-04 | Blue light sweeping a wet hawthorn hedge. The vehicle stays out of frame | B |
| S1-05 | Police tape across the foreground, out of focus; the trodden ring beyond | B |

The bicycle in S1-01 does the whole turn on its own, and it does it without a
child in frame.

## 09:34–13:00 · the forensic pathologist
*"retsmediciner og kig på lig"*

The hardest segment and the one that most needs the rails. Nothing here shows a
body. The sequence is about **procedure** — cold rooms, steel, instruments,
routine — and procedure is more unsettling than anatomy.

| | Shot | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **★ S2-01** | **A wet gloved hand lifting an instrument from a green cloth. 85mm at f/1.8, hard overhead 4000K, the room falling away into green-grey.** | **B, 5s** |
| S2-02 | Empty institutional corridor, one heavy door ajar at the end. Slow push | A, 6s |
| S2-03 | Empty steel table, drain slot, a film of water. A drop travels the slot | B |
| S2-04 | A radiograph on a backlit panel, abstracted and out of focus | B, 4s |

S2-03 is the one to watch in review. An empty table, held, is more effective
than anything on it — and it is the shot most likely to be argued about.

## 15:30–17:09 · the accelerant and the tyre print
*"tændvæske i bål, dæk aftryk"*

Two heroes, because Lasse's note names two distinct things: the fire was
helped, and something drove away. This is the segment where the picture can
carry actual evidence-reasoning, and where it is most tempting — and most
forbidden — to fake a forensic photograph.

| | Shot | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **★ S3-01** | **Flame running fast along one fuel-wet timber while the wood beneath is still black and unlit. 85mm, low, flame the only light source.** | **B, 5s** |
| S3-02 | Lighter-fluid bottle on its side in wet grass, label turned away, cap gone | B, 4s |
| S3-03 | Macro: liquid soaking into charred grain, wet edge advancing, faint iridescence | B, 4s |
| **★ S3-04** | **A tyre track deep in wet clay, tread sharp and holding water, evidence scale beside it. Low raking sun — the one moment of direct light in the episode.** | **B, 5s** |
| S3-05 | The farm track running away between hedges into flat fog. Slow push | A, 6s |

S3-01 is the only warm frame in the programme, which is exactly why the style
bible forbids amber everywhere else.

## 20:17–21:05 · the interview
*"sveder, drabsmænd, brandbart væske"*

The segment that would traditionally need actors. It does not.

| | Shot | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **★ S4-01** | **Two hands on scratched laminate, fingers interlaced too tightly, knuckles pale, an untouched cup of water. Above the wrists, only a dark unlit mass.** | **B, 5s** |
| S4-02 | The room before anyone is in it. A blade of blind-light creeps across the table | A, 6s |
| S4-03 | Wall recorder, one red indicator, dust on the housing | B, 4s |
| S4-04 | A dented jerrycan in a concrete garage, dust in a shaft of daylight | B |

S4-01 is the proof of the whole thesis. It is a shot of a suspect under
pressure with no suspect in it, and it is a stronger frame than a face would
have been.

## What to look for when the frames come back

In review order, because this is what decides whether the format works:

1. **Do the five heroes look like the same programme?** If not, the look is not
   locked hard enough — that is a style bible problem, not a model problem.
2. **Does S4-01 land without a face?** If it does, the thesis holds and
   character consistency never becomes a cost.
3. **Does S2-03 survive a 5-second hold?** Generated stills often fall apart on
   a hold; that determines cutting rhythm for the whole episode.
4. **Is S3-01 the only warm frame?** Amber leaking elsewhere is the earliest
   sign of drift.
